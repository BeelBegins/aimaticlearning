Accessibility

Last updated: 13 October 2026

We aim to provide an accessible learning experience and treat learners fairly.

Report a barrier or ask for an adjustment

Use the Contact page and choose "Accessibility", or email hello@examic.study. Tell us which page or tool you were using and what got in the way. We will consider reasonable adjustments and explain available support.

Official SQE assessments

Examic platform support does not arrange adjustments for official SQE assessments. Candidates must use the official assessment provider's process.
