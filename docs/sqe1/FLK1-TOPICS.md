# FLK1 coverage checklist

From the SRA FLK1 PDF applicable from 1 September 2026 (April 2026 review).
Official file: [official/sqe1-flk1-from-1-september-2026.pdf](official/sqe1-flk1-from-1-september-2026.pdf).

Examic course map is in [MOCK-BUILD.md](MOCK-BUILD.md). Use this list as the
**do-not-miss** heading set. A sitting samples; three mocks together should

Ethics can wrap any heading. Taxation only under Business Law and Practice.

## Business Law and Practice — Session 1

Excludes Listing, Prospectus, Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules and
any other FCA, London Stock Exchange, market rules or codes.

- Business and organisational characteristics: sole trader / partnership / LLP / private and unlisted public companies
- Legal personality and limited liability (same vehicles)
- Incorporate a company / form a partnership or LLP and other start-up steps
  - constitutional documents
  - Companies House filing
- Finance
  - funding: debt and equity (including redemption and buyback of shares; excluding other share-capital reductions and CA 2006 financial assistance)
  - types of security
  - distribution of profits and gains
  - financial records, information and accounting requirements
- Corporate governance and compliance
  - directors’ and shareholders’ rights, duties and powers
  - company decision-making and meetings: procedure, disclosure, approval
  - documentary, record-keeping, statutory filing and disclosure
  - appointment and removal of directors
  - minority shareholder protection
- Partnership decision-making and authority
  - Partnership Act 1890 procedures and authority
  - common partnership-agreement provisions
- Insolvency (corporate and personal)
  - CVA / IVA, bankruptcy, administration, fixed-asset receivership, voluntary and compulsory liquidation
  - claw-back: preferences, transactions at an undervalue, fraudulent and wrongful trading, setting aside a floating charge
  - order of priority for distribution to creditors
- Taxation — business
  - Income Tax: chargeable persons (employees, sole traders, partners, shareholders, lenders, debenture holders); basis of charge; calculation and collection in England and in Wales; anti-avoidance
  - Capital Gains Tax: sole traders, partners, shareholders; calculation, collection, anti-avoidance
  - Corporation Tax: income profits and chargeable gains; payment/collection; distributions to shareholders; outline anti-avoidance
  - VAT: scope, supply, input/output, registration and invoices, returns/payment, records
  - Inheritance Tax: business property relief only

## Dispute Resolution — Session 1

- Arbitration vs mediation vs litigation as the appropriate mechanism
- Preliminary: parties, causes of action, limitation (contract and tort)
- Practice Direction — pre-action conduct
- Pre-action protocols: purpose, and consequences of non-compliance
- Applicable law: which country’s laws apply to a contractual or tortious claim issued in England and Wales
- Practice Direction — Welsh language in civil proceedings
- Where to start: High Court vs county court; specialist courts
- Issuing and serving
  - claim form; adding/removing/substituting parties
  - service within the jurisdiction
  - service outside (with or without permission) and valid service in another jurisdiction
  - deemed dates and time limits; alternative method of service
- Responding
  - admission; acknowledgment and defence/counterclaim
  - disputing jurisdiction
  - default judgment and setting aside
  - discontinuance and settlement
  - time limits
- Statements of case
  - claim form, particulars, defence (contract or tort)
  - reply, Part 20 claim, defence to Part 20
  - further information; amendments
- Interim applications: procedure; summary judgment; interim payments; interim injunctions
- Case management
  - overriding objective; track allocation
  - directions on fast, **intermediate**, or multi-track
  - non-compliance, sanctions, relief
  - costs and case management conferences
- Evidence: relevance, hearsay, admissibility; burden and standard of proof
  - experts: opinion, duties, single joint expert, discussions
  - witnesses: statements, affidavits
- Disclosure and inspection
  - standard, orders, specific, pre-action and non-party, electronic
  - privilege and without prejudice; waiver
- Trial
  - summoning witnesses; pre-trial checklists (listing questionnaires) and hearings; trial bundles
  - procedure, modes of address, etiquette, leading vs non-leading questions
  - nature and effect of judgment
- Costs: budgeting; inter-partes (interim and final); non-party; Part 36 and other offers; security for costs; fixed and assessed costs
- Appeals: permission, destination, grounds
- Enforcement of money judgments: oral examination / information from debtor; methods of enforcement

## Legal Services — Session 1

- SRA principles and risk-based regulation
- Reserved legal activities
- Professional indemnity insurance
- Other regulated providers of legal services
- Equality Act 2010
- Money laundering
  - purpose and scope of AML legislation, including international context
  - when suspicion must be reported
  - to whom, when, and the procedure
  - POCA 2002 direct and non-direct involvement offences and defences
  - due diligence
- Financial services
  - regulatory framework and authorisation as it applies to solicitors’ firms
  - specified investments, specified activities, exemptions
  - FSMA 2000 and related secondary legislation applied to a solicitor’s work
- Funding
  - private retainer; CFAs; DBAs; fixed fees
  - eligibility for criminal and civil legal aid
  - third-party funding; legal expenses insurance

## Tort — Session 2

- Negligence
  - duty (general and professional standard) and breach
  - causation (single and multiple)
  - remoteness and loss
  - remedies for personal injury and death
  - pure economic loss from negligent act or misstatement
  - psychiatric harm
  - employers’ primary liability (common law)
- Defences: volenti; contributory negligence; illegality
- Vicarious liability
- Occupiers’ Liability Act 1957 (visitors) and 1984 (non-visitors); defences; exclusion of liability
- Product liability: negligence and Consumer Protection Act 1987
- Nuisance: public and private; *Rylands v Fletcher*; damages, injunctions, defences

## Contract Law — Session 2

- Formation: offer and acceptance; consideration; intention; certainty; capacity
- Parties: privity; rights of third parties
- Terms
  - express; incorporation; implied by common law and statute
  - exemption clauses
  - interpretation of terms
  - classification: conditions, warranties, innominate terms
  - variation
- Vitiating factors: misrepresentation; mistake; unfair terms; duress and undue influence; illegality
- Termination: expiry or specified event; breach; frustration; basic restitution / unjust enrichment on termination
- Remedies: damages; liquidated sums and penalties; specific performance; injunctions; mitigation; indemnities; guarantees
- Causation and remoteness

## Legal System — Session 2

Blueprint treats this as **one** 14–20% area.

### Legal System of England and Wales and sources of law

- Courts: judicial function / judiciary; hierarchy, appeals, jurisdiction; rights of audience
- Precedent
- Primary legislation: structure of an Act of Parliament; structure of an Act of Senedd Cymru
- Statutory interpretation: literal, golden, mischief, purposive; presumptions; aids; interpretation of Senedd legislation
- Application of Senedd Cymru and Westminster legislation to England and to Wales

### Constitutional and Administrative law and EU law

- Core institutions: Parliament and sovereignty; central government and accountability; devolved institutions and Westminster; monarch and the Crown; constitutional conventions
- Prerogative power vs legislation and conventions
- Parliamentary privilege
- Enactment, implementation and repeal of primary and secondary legislation by Westminster, Senedd Cymru and Welsh Ministers
- Legitimacy, separation of powers, rule of law
- Public order: processions; assemblies; breach of the peace
- Judicial review: nature, process, limits, supervisory nature, remedies, challengeable decisions, standing, time limits; grounds (illegality, irrationality, procedural impropriety, legitimate expectation)
- HRA 1998 ss.2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10; Schedule 1 Convention Rights
- Place of EU law in the UK constitution
