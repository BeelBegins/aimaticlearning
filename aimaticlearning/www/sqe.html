Your preparation toolkit

Learn with structure

Bring your SQE1 preparation into one organised online learning environment. Study legal principles, work through practice questions and revisit the topics that need more attention.

Summary notes

Structured notes for each published FLK1 and FLK2 subject, divided into chapters and short lesson sections.

Practice questions

Chapter questions with an explanation for every answer, so you learn from the reasoning and not only the score.

Digital flashcards

Rate each card hard, good or easy, pause a session and return to it later.

Mock feedback

After a mock session, download your score, results by subject area, strongest and weakest topics, and the answer explanations.

Study Buddy (beta)

An AI assistant that answers from the approved material of the lesson you have open. It can be wrong, so check important points against your course notes.

A better study rhythm

Prepare → Practice → Perform

Understand the rule, apply your knowledge, then review your learning, without losing your place or wondering what comes next.

01

Prepare

Build a clear foundation in the legal principles you are studying, with focused notes in manageable lesson sections.

  • Clear chapter structure
  • Readable, distraction-free lessons
  • Your progress saved
02

Practice

Work through legal scenarios and learn from the answer explanations, not just the score.

  • Chapter practice questions
  • Hard, good and easy flashcard ratings
  • Resume safely when you return
03

Perform

Test yourself under timed conditions, then use your mistakes to guide what you revise next.

  • Timed SQE1 mock sessions
  • Results by subject area
  • Weak areas to revise
