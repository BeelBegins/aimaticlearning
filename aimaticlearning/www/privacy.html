Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This page explains what Examic Study collects and why.

What we collect

Your name and email address, your study activity (lessons opened, quiz and mock answers and scores, flashcard progress) and basic technical data such as browser type needed to run the site securely.

How we use it

To run your account, show your progress, send account and course emails, and improve the questions and notes. We do not sell your data.

Who processes it

Our hosting provider stores the data. Email is sent through our email provider. The Study Buddy assistant sends the text of your question to an AI provider to produce an answer.

Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account and study data by emailing the address below. We keep only what the law requires after deletion.

Cookies

We use cookies needed to keep you signed in and to protect the site. We do not use advertising cookies.

Contact

Questions about this page: hello@examic.study.
