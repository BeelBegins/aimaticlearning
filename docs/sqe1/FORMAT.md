# SQE1 format and rules (January 2027)

Target sitting for Examic mocks: **January 2027 onwards** (also July 2027).
Do not build 180-question / ungrouped-session papers.

## What changed on 10 September 2026

Sources: SRA press release
https://referral.sra.org.uk/news/news/changes-sqe1/ and the SQE news item
https://sqe.sra.org.uk/news-item/2026/09/10/important-changes-to-sqe1-for-january-2027-onwards
(session table as published on that page).

| | Until July 2026 | From January 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Questions per FLK | 180 | **170** |
| Questions per session | 90 | **85** |
| Questions across SQE1 | 360 | **340** |
| Time per session | 2 hours 33 minutes | 2 hours 33 minutes (unchanged) |
| Break between sessions | 60 minutes | 60 minutes |
| Subjects in a session | Any subject of that FLK | **Only that session’s grouped subjects** |
| Order within a session | Random / mixed | Random / mixed (unchanged) |
| Ethics | Pervasive | Pervasive — can appear in any session |
| Blueprint (Annex 4) | Unchanged | Unchanged |
| Who it applies to | — | First sitters **and** re-sitters |

Applies to every candidate from January 2027. No legacy 180-question option.

### Session grouping (do not mix across the break)

| Paper | Session | Subject areas |
|---|---|---|
| FLK1 | 1 | Business Law and Practice; Dispute Resolution; Legal Services |
| FLK1 | 2 | Tort; Contract Law; Legal Systems (Legal System of England and Wales; Constitutional and Administrative Law and EU Law) |
| FLK2 | 1 | Wills and the Administration of Estates; Solicitors Accounts (wills context); Trusts Law; Land Law |
| FLK2 | 2 | Property Law and Practice; Solicitors Accounts (property / conveyancing context); Criminal Liability; Criminal Law and Practice |

Ethics and Professional Conduct is **not** a grouped subject. It can appear in
any of the four sessions.

Solicitors Accounts appears in **both** FLK2 sessions, in different contexts.
Revise and sample it twice.

Questions inside a session stay mixed. Do not block all Contract items together.

## Item and delivery rules (specification overview PDF)

- Closed book, computer-based.
- Single best answer: **five options, one correct**. Candidate selects one.
- Functioning legal knowledge at **Threshold Standard Level 3** (newly
  qualified solicitor). Not specialist practice. Not look-up detail.
- SQE1 tests Miller’s pyramid **“knows how”** (Annex 2).
- Realistic client-based and ethical problems.
- Questions may combine any subjects **within that FLK** that arise in
  practice. From January 2027, the combination must still sit inside the
  **session’s** subject list (Ethics excepted).
- Annex 4 uses **single best classification**: an item is counted against its
  primary area even if it also needs another area. Blueprint % is therefore a
  floor on primary tags, not a cap on knowledge used.

### Application styles the spec lists (not exhaustive)

- Identify **and** apply a principle.
- Principle given; identify proper application and/or outcome.
- Can the client legally achieve the desired outcome, and advise.
- Result given; identify why, and/or which principle produced it.
- Calculation using rules, rates, percentages, thresholds.

### Taxation

Examined **only** in:

- Business Law and Practice
- Property Law and Practice
- Wills and the Administration of Estates

Candidates must know that reliefs/exemptions exist and the conditions. Where
the **monetary value**, rate, or threshold is needed, the question provides it.
Long-standing rates may still be assumed; Budget-adjusted figures are given.

### England and Wales

One legal jurisdiction, but some law differs in Wales (including official
status of the Welsh language in proceedings). Candidates must apply sources of
primary and secondary legislation in England and Wales and know where the law
differs. Property tax is SDLT in England and LTT in Wales.

### Legal authorities

Know and use a case name or provision only when it **is** the usual name of
the principle or step (examples in the spec: *Rylands v Fletcher*, CPR Part 36,
Section 25 notice). Otherwise do not require recall of citations.

### Ethics and professional conduct

Pervasive across both FLKs and all subject areas. Test:

- purpose, scope and content of the SRA Principles;
- SRA Code of Conduct for Solicitors (wording used in the assessment for the
  Code for Solicitors, RELs, RFLs and RSLs);
- SRA Code of Conduct for Firms as it applies to managers in authorised firms
  and Compliance Officers.

Scenarios can include SRA warning-notice situations (the spec names NDAs and
SLAPPs).

Annex 4: Ethics and Professional Conduct **and** money-laundering questions
can make up to **20%** of the questions in any SQE1 sitting (FLK1 and FLK2).

### Law cut-off

Four calendar months before the first assessment in the window. January 2027
cut-off: **11 September 2026**. Candidates are tested on the law as it stands
on that date, not on its history. A change that takes effect on the cut-off
date is examinable.

### Scoring (not changed on 10 Sep 2026)

SRA’s published position, not listed among the January 2027 format changes:
each FLK is scaled 0–500 with a pass mark of 300; both FLKs must be passed;
attempts are capped at three per part within six years of the first sitting.
Mocks should report raw and, if we later scale, say so. Do not invent a
different pass mark.

## Annex 4 blueprint (unchanged)

Percentages are of **each 170-question paper**, not of a session. The SRA has
not published per-session percentages. Question counts below are 170 × the
range, rounded.

### FLK1

| Primary classification | % of paper | Questions on a 170 paper |
|---|---:|---|
| Business Law and Practice | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Dispute Resolution | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Contract Law | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Tort | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Legal System of England and Wales, sources of law, Constitutional and Administrative law, EU law | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Legal Services | 12–16% | 20–27 |
| Ethics and Professional Conduct | Pervades | counted in the primary subject |

The six percentages must sum to 100% of 170. Session 1 must contain all BLP,
DR and Legal Services items; Session 2 all Tort, Contract and Legal System
items. Those three session-1 ranges (24–34 + 24–34 + 20–27) must be chosen so
they **sum to 85**. Session-2 ranges must sum to 85.

### FLK2

| Primary classification | % of paper | Questions on a 170 paper |
|---|---:|---|
| Property Law and Practice, including Solicitors Accounts in conveyancing | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Land Law | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Wills and the Administration of Estates, including Solicitors Accounts in wills/estates | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Trusts Law | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Criminal Liability | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Criminal Law and Practice | 14–20% | 24–34 |
| Ethics and Professional Conduct | Pervades | counted in the primary subject |

Session 1 carries Wills (incl. accounts), Trusts, Land — three blueprint
areas summing to 85. Session 2 carries Property (incl. accounts), Criminal
Liability, Criminal Law and Practice — three areas summing to 85.

## Annex 5 — competences SQE1 actually tests

On both FLK1 and FLK2: **A1, A4, A5, B3, B7, D2, D3**.

SQE1 does not test B1/B2/B4/B5/B6 or C1–C3 (those sit in SQE2). Do not build
SQE1 items whose only point is drafting, advocacy, or negotiation skill.

## Annex 6 — Level 3 (item difficulty)

Items should require identifying the relevant principles and applying them
appropriately to the client’s case. Straightforward transactions, including
occasional unfamiliar tasks; not refined specialist work.

## Timing for mocks

- 85 questions in **153 minutes** (~108 seconds each).
- 60-minute break between a day’s two sessions.
- FLK1 and FLK2 on **separate days**.

A subject module assessment (65–150 questions, one course) is **not** this
exam.
