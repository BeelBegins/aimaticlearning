Frequently asked questions

Who is Examic for?

Examic supports people preparing for SQE1 as part of the route to becoming a solicitor in England and Wales.

Does a subscription qualify me as a solicitor?

No. A preparation subscription does not replace the SRA's qualification and admission requirements. Check the current requirements with the SRA.

Is Examic accredited or endorsed by the SRA?

Examic is an independent provider. The SRA does not accredit or endorse SQE preparation providers.

Is SQE2 included?

No. Our initial offering focuses on SQE1. Any SQE2 product will be announced separately.

How does the introductory offer work?

Your first {{ trial_days }} days include every published subject course and {{ free_mock_names }} ({{ free_sessions_per_flk }} FLK1 sessions and {{ free_sessions_per_flk }} FLK2 sessions), with no payment required to start. To keep going, choose {{ monthly_price }} per month or {{ three_month_price }} upfront for three months of full access. Free access never turns into a paid plan by itself.

Do I need a payment card for free access?

No.

What happens if I choose the Monthly plan during the free month?

{{ monthly_price }} is charged at checkout, every mock unlocks straight away and your monthly billing starts that day.

Does access include examination fees?

No. Official assessment fees and booking are separate.

Is personal tutoring included?

No. Access to the online materials does not include one-to-one tuition.

How do I report a mistake?

Email {{ support_email }} or use the contact form and include the subject, lesson or question reference. Do not include confidential client or case information.
