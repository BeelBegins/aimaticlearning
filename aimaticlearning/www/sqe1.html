SQE1 Preparation

Prepare for SQE1 with Examic.study

SQE1 tests functioning legal knowledge: the ability to apply legal principles to practical situations. Examic provides an online environment for studying the subjects and practising the questions included in your plan.

SQE1 includes FLK1 and FLK2. The catalogue below shows the subjects published on Examic today. Your account gives access to the resources included in your plan. Official examination fees, assessment booking and admission to the profession are separate from Examic's service.

SQE2 preparation will be introduced separately. It is not included in the current SQE1 offer.

What you study with

Clearly structured notes

Summary notes for each published subject, focused on the legal knowledge tested in SQE1 and divided into chapters.

Chapter practice questions

Subject questions with an explanation for every answer. Review the reasoning behind each answer rather than relying on memorisation.

Digital flashcards

Focused revision of key legal rules and principles. Rate recall as hard, good or easy and pick up where you stopped.

Performance feedback

After each mock session, download your score, results by subject area, your strongest and weakest topics, and the answer explanations. Use this to decide where to focus your next revision session.

Revision board

Your revision page shows weak areas and chapters to revise, built from your practice results, so you can organise your revision priorities.

Study Buddy (beta)

Ask questions in plain English while you study. Study Buddy answers from the approved material of the lesson you have open. AI responses can contain errors; check important points against your course notes.

Flexible online learning

Study around work and family commitments with access from your laptop, tablet or phone.
