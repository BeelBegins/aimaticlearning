## Authority

1. Live SRA pages and PDFs linked in `SOURCES.md`.
2. This pack (extracted 2026-09-17 from those sources).
3. Current Examic question bank on `lms.aimatic.tech`.

SRA samples from the specification. A proper mock therefore:

- hits **every Annex 4 blueprint area** in each FLK paper at the stated
  percentages;
- uses **January 2027 session grouping**, timing, and item style;
- treats Ethics as pervasive;
- spreads topic headings across sittings so nothing in FLK1/FLK2 is
  systematically excluded.

It does **not** require every sub-heading in a single 170-question paper.

Do not copy Kaplan papers. Kaplan-hard means application-level items from
our reviewed bank, not Kaplan wording or keys.
