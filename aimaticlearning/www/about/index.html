About Us

About Examic

Examic.study is an online learning platform operated by EXAMIC LTD, supporting aspiring solicitors preparing for the Solicitors Qualifying Examination in England and Wales.

Our current focus is SQE1, with structured resources for both FLK1 and FLK2. We bring summary notes, practice questions, digital flashcards and timed mock exams together in one accessible platform. Learners can also use Study Buddy, an AI study assistant, and the feedback from each mock to guide their preparation.

Our aim is to make legal preparation clear, practical and affordable, helping learners understand the law, apply it to realistic scenarios and identify areas that need further revision.

Content queries

If something in a note, question, explanation or flashcard looks wrong, report a content query with the subject, lesson or question reference. The responsible team will assess it and correct the content where needed.

Study Buddy answers from the approved material of the lesson you have open. Its answers are generated by AI, can contain errors and are not a substitute for your course notes.
