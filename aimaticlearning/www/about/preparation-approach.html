Our Preparation Approach

Understand legal principles. Practise applying them. Review your progress.

Build understanding

Work through the legal principles covered by your course. Use the explanations to connect the rules with the situations in which they apply.

Practise applying the law

Use practice questions to examine your understanding and identify gaps. Review the reasoning behind answers rather than relying on memorisation alone.

Revisit and reinforce

Return to challenging subjects and repeat your practice. A consistent routine can help you make productive use of your preparation time.

Prepare with realistic expectations

Practice results help guide revision; they do not predict your official examination result. Learners remain responsible for their own preparation, assessment registration and checking current official requirements.
