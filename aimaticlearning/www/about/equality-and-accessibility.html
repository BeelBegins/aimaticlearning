About Us

Equality and Accessibility

We aim to provide an accessible learning experience and treat learners fairly. Use the Contact page and choose "Accessibility" to report an accessibility barrier or request an adjustment. We will consider reasonable adjustments and explain available support.

Examic platform support does not arrange adjustments for official SQE assessments. Candidates must use the official assessment provider's process.

Read our accessibility statement for how the website is built and what we know needs work.
